How it works

The whole loop, stage by stage.

Six stages turn one idea into thirteen checked posts. Nothing here is a black box — each stage leaves a record you can read in the loop's report.

01

Ingest

You paste a topic, a draft or a link. Links are fetched and stripped down to their readable article text, so the loop works from what the page actually says.

02

Brief

Gemini distils the source into one platform-neutral brief: a one-sentence thesis, the audience, 3–7 key points, a handful of hooks, a call to action, and a list of facts copied from the source. Your brand voice is added here.

03

Fan-out

Thirteen writers run in parallel — one per platform — each handed that platform's rules: its hard character limit, its hashtag range, its tone, its format, and any required field such as a YouTube title or a subreddit.

04

Check and repair

Code, not the model, decides whether a post fits. X is measured with its weighted count (links are 23, emoji and CJK are 2), Bluesky by graphemes, everything else by characters. Hashtags, titles, required fields and banned phrases are checked too. A failed draft goes back to the model with the exact errors, up to three times; after that it is trimmed to fit and flagged for you.

05

Review

Every post lands in the console as a draft with a live character meter. Edit any field and it is re-checked as you type. Approve, skip, or ask for a fresh rewrite of a single platform.

06

Publish

Approved posts go to your publishing provider — Zernio by default, Ayrshare as an alternative — to the account connected for each platform. Post now or pick a time. Until you choose live, publishing is only a preview of exactly what would be sent.

Then it goes around again

Autopilot is the loop, on a timer.

Queue up ideas once. At the time you choose — every day, every few days — autopilot takes the next idea and runs all six stages.

  • Review mode — drafts wait in the console for your approval.
  • Publish mode — posts that pass every check go out on their own; flagged ones wait.

Being honest about scope

What the loop does today, and what is next.

Working today

  • Topic, draft or link in
  • Brief with source-only facts and brand voice
  • Thirteen posts with enforced limits and repair
  • Edit, approve, skip, rewrite one platform
  • Preview, publish now or schedule via Zernio or Ayrshare
  • Autopilot queue with review or publish mode
  • Printable report for every loop

On the roadmap

  • Rendered images and carousels for Instagram and Pinterest
  • Short vertical videos for TikTok, Shorts and Snapchat
  • Niche scan: learn from what already works for similar accounts
  • Best-time scheduling per platform
  • Post analytics pulled back into each report

Instagram, TikTok, YouTube, Pinterest and Snapchat need media to publish. Until rendering ships, attach media yourself in your provider or post those five manually.
