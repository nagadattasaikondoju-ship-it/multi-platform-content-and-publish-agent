FAQ

The long answers.

What is this — an app, or a file?

A web app. Sign in with Google and everything runs in your browser. Every loop is saved: you can reopen it, edit it, publish it later, and print its report.

Do I need any API keys or developer accounts?

No. Sign in with Google, then connect your social accounts from the Accounts page using each platform's own sign-in.

What does it cost to publish?

Nothing. Publishing is never metered, on any plan.

What if I don't like a post?

Edit it directly — it is re-checked as you type — or rewrite just that platform. Skipped posts are never published.

Does it make things up?

The brief only carries facts found in your source, and the writers are told not to add any. If you paste a topic with no facts, the posts will not contain statistics.

What about Instagram, TikTok and YouTube?

Their captions, titles and scripts are written and checked today. Those platforms also need an image or video, which Grow it does not render yet — post those five manually for now.
