FAQ

The long answers.

What is this — an app, or a file?

A web app with a console. Every loop is saved: you can reopen it, edit it, publish it later, and print its report.

Why do I need a Gemini key and a Zernio key?

Gemini writes the brief and the posts. Zernio holds the connections to your social accounts and does the actual posting. Keys stay in your server's environment; the console only shows whether each one is set.

What does it cost to publish?

Nothing from Grow it. Zernio bills per connected profile per month, so a post costs the same whether you send one this month or two hundred.

What if I don't like a post?

Edit it directly — it is re-checked as you type — or rewrite just that platform. Skipped posts are never published.

Does it make things up?

The brief only carries facts found in your source, and the writers are told not to add any. If you paste a topic with no facts, the posts will not contain statistics.

What about Instagram, TikTok and YouTube?

Their captions, titles and scripts are written and checked today. Those platforms also need an image or video, which Grow it does not render yet — attach one in Zernio or post those manually.
