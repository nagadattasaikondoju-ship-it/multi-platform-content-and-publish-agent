Before you paste anything

The questions people ask first.

What do I actually paste?

Anything with an idea in it: a one-line topic, a rough draft, a full blog post, or a link to an article. Links are fetched and reduced to their readable text before the brief is written.

Will it post without asking me?

No. Every publish is a preview until you choose “live”. Autopilot has two modes: review (drafts wait for you) and publish (clean drafts go out on their own). It starts in review.

What happens when a post is too long?

The validator rejects it and sends the exact problem back to the model — “302 characters, the limit is 280, cut 22”. After three attempts it trims the post to fit and flags it for your review instead of posting it.

Which accounts can it post to?

Any of the thirteen you connect in Zernio (or Ayrshare). Platforms without a connected account are skipped, never guessed.
